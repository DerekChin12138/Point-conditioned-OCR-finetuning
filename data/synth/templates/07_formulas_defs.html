Loss and evaluation notes

For point-conditioned transcription we maximize the likelihood of the target block tokens given image \(I\) and crosshair \(c\).

\( \mathcal{L}_{\mathrm{SFT}}=-\sum_t\log p_\theta(y_t\mid y_{<t},I,c) \)

Hit rate

A prediction counts as a hit when normalized edit similarity to the gold block exceeds a threshold \(\tau\) (default \(0.85\)).

\( \mathrm{sim}(a,b)=1-\frac{\mathrm{ED}(a,b)}{\max(|a|,|b|)} \)

Over-extraction flags outputs that are much longer than the gold block or contain multiple section headings.
