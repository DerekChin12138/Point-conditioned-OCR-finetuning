TECHNOLOGY REVIEW

THE CURSOR PROBLEM

Why full-page dump fails in the wild

Readers skim interfaces the way they skim magazines: eyes jump, hands hover, attention narrows to a single column inch.

A parser that always narrates the entire spread cannot power hover translation without flooding the user.

Crosshair-conditioned fine-tuning reframes the task as block selection plus transcription.

Synthetic magazine layouts stress multi-column reading order and tight gutter negatives.

When the marker sits between columns, the correct answer is often empty — not a blend of both stories.
