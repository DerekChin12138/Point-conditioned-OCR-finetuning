A mid-density reading column

This layout aims for about forty to fifty percent text occupancy in a focused window: enough paragraphs to look like a real article, enough margin that the mark can still land on blank paper.

Point-conditioned OCR should return one semantic block. The block is a paragraph or a short heading-plus-list unit, never the whole page.

Why occupancy matters

Sparse kanban and invoice templates left most of the screenshot empty, so the model barely saw body text. Dense github dumps overlapped boxes and counted past 100%.

We therefore add reader pages that sit in the missing 40–60% band and let the filler CSS walk the rest of the 30–70% range.

Chrome remains a third layer. It should not steal the occupancy budget from the article body.

Sampling

Each labeled text box is split into five inner-80% regions. Every region contributes two random points.

Empty labels stay far from ink, or sit on formulas, tables, code, and placeholder figures.

Junction gutters between two units are not used in this SFT mix.
