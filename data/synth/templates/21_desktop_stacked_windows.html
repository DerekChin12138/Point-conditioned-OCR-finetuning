Point-conditioned block OCR on busy desktops

Guide · Updated 2026-03-11 · Training data notes

Real users hover across overlapping windows: a browser article behind a sticky note, a terminal peeking under a finder panel. The model must ignore occluded text and chrome.

Synthetic desktops stack windows with explicit z-index. Only visible, labeled blocks receive positive points; title bars and docks stay unlabeled for negatives.

Prefer dense copy: multiple paragraphs, side lists, and compact tables so the screenshot is information-rich rather than a sparse card on empty wallpaper.
